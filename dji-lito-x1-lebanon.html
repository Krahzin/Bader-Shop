Lito X1 or Lito 1?

Choose the Lito X1 if you care about image quality: the larger sensor, HDR and D-Log M colour make a visible difference at sunset and in the mountains. The Lito 1 is the lower-cost option with the same flight time and safety sensors. Both use the same accessories.

RC-N3 or RC 2 combo?

The RC 2 combo has a built-in bright screen, so you don't need your phone and calls won't interrupt your flight. The standard combo uses your phone as the screen and costs less.
