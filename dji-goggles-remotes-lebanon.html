Why use a sunhood in Lebanon?

On sunny days on the coast or in the mountains, glare makes the screen hard to read, and you can lose sight of your drone's position. A fold-up or solid sunhood for the RC 2, or the RC-N1 sunhood for your phone, makes the image clear and safer to fly.
