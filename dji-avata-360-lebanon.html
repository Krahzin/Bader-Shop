Why a 360° FPV drone?

With a normal FPV drone you have to point the camera at the action. With the Avata 360 you capture everything around the drone and choose the angle later in editing, so one flight over Jounieh bay or Faqra gives you dozens of shots. It is perfect for real estate, events and travel videos.

Protecting the 360° lenses

The two lenses stick out on both sides, so the side protection pieces are essential. Add landing feet for rough ground, a battery case for safe transport and a hard case for the whole kit.
