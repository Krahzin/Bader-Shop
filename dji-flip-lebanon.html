DJI Flip vs DJI Neo 2

Both take off from your hand, but the Flip has the bigger 1/1.3-inch sensor, longer flight time and better image quality in low light, while the Neo 2 is smaller and cheaper. If image quality matters most, choose the Flip. Read our full DJI Neo 2 vs DJI Flip comparison.

The Flip folds into an unusual shape, so a fitted case (compact or large) makes it easy to carry. A gimbal protector keeps the camera safe in your bag, the landing gear lifts it off rough ground, and the payload mount lets you carry light items for fun drops.
