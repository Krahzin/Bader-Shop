Before you order

Are your used drones tested?

Yes. Every used drone is checked before it is sold, and you can test it with us before you pay. Battery cycle counts are listed on the product when we have them.

Do drones come with a warranty?

Our used drones come with a short shop warranty, and new sealed drones come with the standard DJI warranty. We confirm the warranty for your drone on WhatsApp.

Which drone is best for beginners?

A DJI Neo or a DJI Mini is a great start: light, simple and forgiving. If you want obstacle sensing to help avoid crashes, look at the Mini 4 Pro, Mini 5 Pro or Lito X1.

What is a Fly More Combo?

DJI’s bundle that adds extra batteries, a charging hub, spare propellers and a carry bag. It is usually the best value if you fly often, because one battery gives around 20 to 35 minutes.

Do you buy or trade in used drones?

Message us on WhatsApp with your drone model, its condition and photos, and we will let you know if we can buy it or take it in trade.

What if I crash my drone?

Bring it to us. Broken propellers, arms and many other parts can be replaced, and we will tell you on WhatsApp what can be fixed.
