Mini 3 or Mini 3 Pro?

Both share the same great camera. The Mini 3 Pro adds forward, backward and downward obstacle sensing and ActiveTrack, so it is safer near trees and buildings. The Mini 3 is cheaper and ideal for open landscapes. Most of our accessories fit both.

Our favourite Mini 3 accessories

The propeller guards are a must for beginners, the landing gear protects the gimbal on rough ground, and the rugged case keeps the drone, remote and batteries safe on hikes and trips around Lebanon.

Flying the Mini 3 in Lebanon

  • Flights in Lebanon need a permit from the Lebanese Army: read our guide to drone laws in Lebanon.
  • Stay well away from airports, military sites and checkpoints, and never fly over crowds.
  • Mountain winds in areas like the Cedars and Koura can be strong; check the wind and keep battery for the flight home.
  • Ask permission before filming people, private property or events.
