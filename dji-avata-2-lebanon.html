Flying FPV in Lebanon

Lebanon has amazing FPV spots: the Raouche rocks, the Batroun coast, the Qadisha Valley and the Bekaa vineyards. FPV drones fly close to terrain, so protect yours with full protection frames and a gimbal protector, and carry a spare original body shell in case of a hard crash. Always fly with a spotter when you are wearing goggles.

Avata 2 Fly More Combo or drone only?

If you are new to FPV, the Fly More Combo includes the goggles, RC Motion 3, three batteries and a charging hub. If you already own DJI goggles and a motion controller, the drone only option saves you a lot of money.

Flying the Avata 2 in Lebanon

  • Flights in Lebanon need a permit from the Lebanese Army: read our guide to drone laws in Lebanon.
  • Stay well away from airports, military sites and checkpoints, and never fly over crowds.
  • Mountain winds in areas like the Cedars and Koura can be strong; check the wind and keep battery for the flight home.
  • Ask permission before filming people, private property or events.
