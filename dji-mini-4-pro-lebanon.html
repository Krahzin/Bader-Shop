Which DJI Mini 4 Pro version should you buy?

The Mini 4 Pro comes in three main packages. The standard version with the RC-N2 uses your phone as the screen and is the cheapest way in. The DJI RC 2 version has a bright built-in screen, so you don't drain your phone or get interrupted by calls mid-flight. The Fly More Combo adds three batteries, a charging hub and a shoulder bag, which is the best value if you plan to film trips around Lebanon, since one battery rarely covers a full shoot.

Flying the Mini 4 Pro in Lebanon

Lebanon is a great place to fly a Mini 4 Pro: the coastline from Batroun to Tyre, the Qadisha Valley, the Cedars and the Bekaa all look stunning from above. A few tips from flying here:

  • Mountain winds in the Cedars and Koura can be strong; check wind speed before take-off and keep battery reserves for the flight home.
  • Stay well away from Beirut airport, military sites and checkpoints, and don't fly over crowds.
  • Ask permission before filming private property, weddings or events.
  • Use ND filters on sunny coastal days for smooth, cinematic video.
