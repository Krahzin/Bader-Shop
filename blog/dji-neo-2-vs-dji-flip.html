DJI Neo 2 vs DJI Flip: Which Should You Buy?

Two palm-launch DJI drones for selfies and vlogs. We compare camera, flight time, safety, size and price in Lebanon to help you choose.

The DJI Neo 2 and DJI Flip are both made for people who want to film themselves: you launch them from your hand and they follow you automatically. But they are quite different drones. Here's how they compare.

Quick verdict

  • Choose the DJI Neo 2 if you want the smallest, lightest drone for selfies, sports and FPV fun.
  • Choose the DJI Flip if image quality and flight time matter most, especially for travel and sunset shots.

Camera and video

The Flip has a much larger 1/1.3-inch 48 MP sensor with 4K/60fps HDR video, so it handles sunsets, shadows and low light far better. The Neo 2 uses a smaller 1/2-inch 12 MP sensor, but it now has a 2-axis gimbal and shoots 4K up to 100 fps, which is great for slow motion.

Size and weight

  • Neo 2: about 151 g, truly pocket-sized
  • Flip: under 249 g, folds flat but is noticeably bigger

Safety and obstacle avoidance

The Flip has full-coverage propeller guards, so it is very safe to fly close to people. The Neo 2 also has guards and adds a forward LiDAR sensor to avoid obstacles in front of it.

Flight time

  • Neo 2: up to about 19 minutes per battery
  • Flip: up to about 31 minutes per battery

For a day trip, the Flip needs fewer battery swaps. Either way, a Fly More Combo with three batteries is worth it.

Control options

Both can be flown from your palm with gestures or the app. The Neo 2 can also be flown with DJI goggles and the RC Motion 3 for immersive FPV flight, which the Flip doesn't support.

Price in Lebanon

The Neo 2 is usually the cheaper of the two, while the Flip costs more but gives you a bigger sensor and longer flights. Prices change with each new shipment, so message us on WhatsApp for the latest price of both.

Our recommendation

For most people who mainly film themselves and their friends, the Neo 2 is the more fun and portable choice. If you want the best footage from a small, safe drone, the Flip is worth the extra size.
