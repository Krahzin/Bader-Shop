Best Beginner Drone to Buy in Lebanon (2026)

The best first DJI drones you can buy in Lebanon right now, from budget to premium, with what each one is good at.

Buying your first drone in Lebanon? Here are the DJI drones we recommend to beginners, sorted from budget to premium. All of them have GPS, automatic Return to Home and are easy to learn on. Prices change with each shipment, so message us on WhatsApp for the latest price.

Budget: DJI Neo or DJI Mini 2

  • DJI Neo: tiny, safe and fun. Perfect for selfies and learning, but video is basic and it struggles in wind.
  • DJI Mini 2: a real camera drone with a 3-axis gimbal, 4K video and around 30 minutes per battery. The best value for landscapes.

See DJI Neo and DJI Mini 2.

Mid-range: Mini 3 Pro, Neo 2 or Flip

  • DJI Mini 3 Pro: big 1/1.3-inch sensor, vertical shooting and obstacle sensing. A favourite for content creators.
  • DJI Neo 2: palm-sized with a gimbal, LiDAR and 4K/100fps.
  • DJI Flip: foldable vlog drone with full propeller guards and great image quality.

Compare them in our DJI Neo 2 vs Flip guide.

Premium: Lito X1 or Mini 4 Pro

  • DJI Lito X1: DJI's newest beginner drone, with obstacle sensing in every direction, forward LiDAR and a 1/1.3-inch HDR camera.
  • DJI Mini 4 Pro: the pro choice in the mini class, with omnidirectional sensing and excellent video.

See DJI Lito X1 and DJI Mini 4 Pro.

Want to try FPV?

The DJI Avata 2 is the easiest way into first-person flying. See Avata 2 in Lebanon.

What else to buy with your first drone

  • Propeller guards for your first flights
  • Landing gear for beaches and rough ground
  • A case to protect the drone in your bag
  • A Fly More Combo or extra batteries, since one battery rarely covers a full outing
