Must-Have Accessories for Your DJI Mini Drone

Propeller guards, cases, landing gear, sunhoods and more: the accessories that protect your DJI Mini and make every flight easier.

You just got a DJI Mini. It flies beautifully, it fits in your hand, and it cost real money. The right accessories keep it safe, make it easier to carry, and help you get better footage. Here are the ones we recommend for the Mini 2 / 2SE / 4K, Mini 3 / 3 Pro, Mini 4 Pro and Mini 5 Pro.

1. Propeller Guards

The single most useful accessory for new pilots. Guards stop the propellers from hitting trees, walls and people, and they save you from bent props after a light bump. Use them for your first flights, indoors, or close to obstacles.

  • Tip: guards add a little weight and catch more wind. Fly them on calm days and expect slightly shorter flight time.
  • Want to fly at dusk? Our glow-in-the-dark guards make the drone easy to spot in low light.

2. A Proper Carry Case

Most damage happens in the bag, not in the air. A shaped case holds the drone, controller and batteries in place so the gimbal and arms don't get knocked around. Pick a drone + controller case if you carry everything together, or a compact one if you travel light.

3. Landing Gear

Landing gear lifts the drone a few centimetres off the ground. That keeps sand, dust and wet grass away from the camera and motors, and makes taking off from beaches, rocks or uneven mountain ground much safer.

4. Gimbal and Camera Protection

The gimbal is the most fragile (and most expensive) part of your Mini. A gimbal cover or camera protector clips on during transport and comes off in seconds before you fly. Never take off with it on.

5. Propeller Clips

Small but important. Clips hold the folded propellers together so they don't bend or crack in your bag, and they make packing faster.

6. Controller Sunhood

In the Lebanese sun, your controller screen can be almost impossible to read. A sunhood for the DJI RC 2 or RC-N1 blocks the glare so you can actually see your shot and your battery level.

7. Battery Accessories

A battery holder keeps spare batteries organised and protected, and a battery removal tool (Mini 2 / 2SE / 4K) makes swapping easier. For longer flights on the Mini 3 / 4 Pro, ask us about the Battery Plus. Remember to check your local weight rules before using it.

8. AirTag / Smart Tag Mount

If your drone ever goes down in a valley or a field, a tracker can be the difference between finding it and losing it. The mount holds an AirTag or smart tag securely without blocking sensors.

9. Just for Fun: Stands and Payload Baskets

A stand turns your drone into a display piece on your desk when it's not flying. A small payload basket lets you drop very light items for fun. Only carry tiny loads, keep away from people, and never fly over crowds.

Our Starter Kit Recommendation

If you only buy three things: propeller guards, a carry case and a controller sunhood. Add landing gear if you fly on beaches or in the mountains.
