How to Choose Your First Drone: A Step-by-Step Guide

FPV or cinematic, beginner or experienced: a step-by-step guide to picking the right first drone, from speed and camera to safety and controllers.

Buying your first drone can feel overwhelming. FPV, cinematic, beginner, pro — what does it all mean? This guide walks you through every decision, one step at a time.

Step 1: Decide What You Want to Do

Ask yourself: Do I want to fly for fun, or film beautiful footage?

  • FPV (First-Person View): You wear goggles and fly like you're in the cockpit. Fast, immersive, thrilling. Great for indoor flying and freestyle.
  • Cinematic: You want smooth, stable, high-quality video. Great for travel, landscapes, and vlogs.
  • Both: Some drones can do both — like the DJI Neo with the right accessories.

Step 2: Match the Drone to Your Experience Level

  • Total beginner: Look for palm takeoff, automatic shots, and propeller guards. The DJI Neo is the easiest starting point.
  • Some experience: You want better camera quality, longer flight time, and more speed. The DJI Mini 4 Pro or Lito series are great next steps.
  • Ready for FPV: You want goggles, a motion controller, and serious speed. The DJI Avata 2 is a dedicated FPV drone built for agile, fast flight.

Step 3: Consider Speed and Performance

Speed matters depending on your goals. Here are the Sport mode speeds for every drone in this guide:

  • DJI Neo: Max horizontal speed of 28.8 km/h in Sport mode.
  • DJI Mini 4 Pro: Max horizontal speed of 57.6 km/h in Sport mode.
  • DJI Lito Series (Lito 1 & Lito X1): Max horizontal speed of 64.8 km/h in Sport mode.
  • DJI Avata 2: Max horizontal speed of 57.6 km/h in Sport mode, and up to 97.2 km/h in Manual mode for experienced FPV pilots.

Step 4: Camera and Video Quality

  • Beginner: 4K/30fps is plenty. Neo delivers this.
  • Intermediate: Look for 4K/60fps, HDR, and larger sensors. Mini 4 Pro and Lito X1 offer this.
  • Advanced: 10-bit D-Log M, 8K, and adjustable aperture. Lito X1 and higher-end models.

Step 5: Flight Time and Range

  • DJI Neo: 12–14 minutes of flight time.
  • Mini 4 Pro and Lito series: 30+ minutes.
  • FPV range: Depends on goggles and controller. The Avata 2 and Neo FPV Combo work well for close-range immersion.

Step 6: Safety Features

  • Obstacle avoidance: Mini 4 Pro and Lito series have omnidirectional sensing.
  • LiDAR: Some advanced drones use LiDAR (Light Detection and Ranging) for highly precise obstacle detection and terrain mapping. It's a step up from standard sensors.
  • Propeller guards: Neo has full guards — safe indoors and around people.
  • Return-to-home: Essential for beginners. Most modern DJI drones include it.

Step 7: Controller and Goggles

  • Phone only: Neo can fly with just your phone.
  • Standard controller or RC2 controller: Mini 4 Pro and Lito series come with either the standard controller or the RC2 controller. The RC2 has a built-in screen.
  • FPV goggles: Needed for immersive flight. DJI Goggles N3 + Motion 3 work with Neo, and the Avata 2 comes with its own FPV setup.
