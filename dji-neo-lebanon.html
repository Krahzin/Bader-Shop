A used Neo is a great budget option because the drone has few moving parts. We check every used drone before selling it: battery health, gimbal, motors and propellers. The Fly More Combo gives you three batteries and a charging hub; the FPV combo adds goggles and the RC Motion 3 for first-person flying.

Our most popular Neo add-ons are the gimbal camera covers (from $5), the roll cage and protection frames for crash-proof indoor flying, a stand to display it at home, and AirTag or Smart Tag mounts so you can find it if it lands somewhere unexpected.

Flying the Neo in Lebanon

  • Flights in Lebanon need a permit from the Lebanese Army: read our guide to drone laws in Lebanon.
  • Stay well away from airports, military sites and checkpoints, and never fly over crowds.
  • Mountain winds in areas like the Cedars and Koura can be strong; check the wind and keep battery for the flight home.
  • Ask permission before filming people, private property or events.
