Must-have Neo 2 accessories

The Neo 2 is light, so wind and small knocks are its weak points. A protection frame or gimbal protector saves the camera from scratches, the landing gear keeps the gimbal off sand and gravel, and a carry case with battery holder keeps everything together in your bag. The LED light helps with orientation at sunset and night flights.

Flying the Neo 2 in Lebanon

  • Flights in Lebanon need a permit from the Lebanese Army: read our guide to drone laws in Lebanon.
  • Stay well away from airports, military sites and checkpoints, and never fly over crowds.
  • Mountain winds in areas like the Cedars and Koura can be strong; check the wind and keep battery for the flight home.
  • Ask permission before filming people, private property or events.

Is the DJI Neo 2 good for beginners?

Yes. It takes off from your palm, follows you automatically and lands back in your hand, and the LiDAR helps it avoid obstacles. It is one of the easiest DJI drones to learn on.

What is the difference between the DJI Neo and Neo 2?

The Neo 2 adds a 2-axis gimbal for smoother video, 4K up to 100 fps instead of 4K/30, forward LiDAR obstacle sensing and a bigger battery, while staying palm-sized.
