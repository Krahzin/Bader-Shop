Returns & Refunds

We only accept returns for defective products.

Defective products

If a product arrives defective, we will replace it or refund you. Please contact us within 7 days of delivery or pickup.

How to request a return

Message us on WhatsApp at 79466176 with your order details and clear photos or a short video showing the defect. We will confirm and arrange the replacement or refund.

Refunds

Approved refunds are issued within 7 days, using the same payment method you used for the order, unless you prefer a replacement.

Return shipping

If the product is defective, we cover the return shipping.

Not accepted

We do not accept returns or exchanges for products that are not defective, or for products damaged through misuse after delivery.

Need help with an order? Message us on WhatsApp and we will get back to you.
